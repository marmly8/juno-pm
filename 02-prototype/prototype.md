## What it demonstrates

A weekly PM digest that groups mock Slack, Jira, and Wishlist signals into recurring themes. Themes are ranked by occurrence and customer tier, with affected accounts, quotes, and clickable source details. Juno presents a draft for PM review and takes no actions.
_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The single-column layout makes the ranked themes easy to scan. Customer tiers and source counts explain why each theme ranks where it does.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Little time to work on it.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Think through where it lives, where it gets triggered so that people look at it and work with it and what actions are taken and how they're tracked from it + how it escalates if the important ones are not being prioritized.
